Support

Éclair Notes is an iPhone app for recording and organizing notes. It requires iOS 26 or later. Recordings and notes are stored in the app on your device; consider your device-backup settings before deleting the app.

Recording and transcription

  • Allow microphone access to record, and obtain any consent required before recording other people.
  • Saved or imported recordings are queued for on-device transcription. Keep the app open while it processes. Recognition requires a supported device and language, permission, and sometimes a model download. If it fails, retry from the recording or enter a transcript manually.
  • On supported devices, Apple Intelligence can prepare an on-device summary. Otherwise the app labels an extractive overview and suggested follow-ups. Review results before relying on or sharing them.
  • Optional providers under AI providers require your own account and API key. Check the destination, terms and possible charges, then confirm an upload for each recording. A separately confirmed connection test sends bundled synthetic speech rather than your recording.
  • Cloud uploads support M4A, MP3, WAV, MP4 and WebM up to 25 MB; other formats or larger files may still work on-device if supported.

Troubleshooting

  1. Check iOS Settings for Éclair Notes microphone and speech-recognition permissions, available storage and the transcription language.
  2. For a provider error, verify the endpoint, deployment or model, API version, key permissions, quota, file type and network. For Microsoft Foundry use the resource endpoint, not the project home URL.
  3. If the problem continues, include your device model, iOS and app versions, language, selected on-device or cloud method, and the error text in your message.

Protect your data: Do not email an audio recording, transcript or API key for troubleshooting. If you choose to share sensitive material, first agree on a suitable secure channel.
