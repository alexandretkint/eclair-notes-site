Privacy Policy

For Éclair Notes · Effective 27 September 2026

Questions or privacy requests about Éclair Notes can be sent to alextkint@me.com. This policy describes the current iPhone app, its optional external transcription, and this support website. It does not replace the privacy terms of a provider you choose.

What stays on your iPhone

Éclair Notes stores your recordings, transcripts, titles, editable summaries, action-item drafts and notes in its local app storage. The current app has no Éclair Notes account, advertising or analytics SDK, or app-operated audio-processing server. On-device transcription is the default. Apple speech recognition may require a language-model download, and Apple Intelligence summaries are only available on supported devices, systems and languages; otherwise the app labels an extractive overview. Microphone permission is needed to record; speech-recognition permission may be needed for on-device transcription.

Optional cloud transcription

You can configure Microsoft Foundry, OpenAI, Deepgram, or a compatible HTTPS endpoint of your choice using your own provider account and API key. No cloud provider is enabled by default. Before an individual recording is sent, the app shows the destination and asks for separate confirmation. It then sends that recording's audio and your provider credential directly to the chosen endpoint to request a transcript. Spoken audio can contain personal or sensitive information about you or other people. A separately confirmed connection test sends bundled synthetic speech and a provider credential; it may incur charges. Saving a recording alone does not send it to these providers.

The key is stored in your device's Keychain; non-secret connection settings are in app preferences. Requests use HTTPS and an ephemeral network session, reject redirects, and limit the audio to 25 MB. Network requests can also reveal technical information such as an IP address to the provider. A provider may associate a request with your account or key. Its retention, training, processing location, security, charges and deletion options depend on that provider, your account configuration and, for a custom endpoint, the service you selected. A resource's configured region does not by itself guarantee where all processing occurs. Review the provider's terms before uploading: Microsoft privacy statement and Azure OpenAI data privacy; OpenAI API data controls; Deepgram privacy policy. For a custom endpoint, consult that operator's terms. The app cannot remove a copy already received by a provider.

Sharing, deletion and backups

Sharing audio or exported notes via the iOS share sheet is your choice; the recipient or destination may retain a copy under its own terms. Deleting a recording in the app removes that recording's local audio and associated app index entry. It cannot delete a copy already shared or uploaded, or remove a device backup. Device backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up. You can remove a saved provider key in app settings. To remove all app-stored content, use the app's deletion controls or delete the app, bearing in mind that backups and copies sent elsewhere may remain.

Support and this website

If you email us, your address, message and any details you choose to include are received through your email provider and used to respond to your request. Please do not send recordings, transcripts or API keys in ordinary support email. This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages; its hosting service may process technical request data under GitHub's privacy statement. This site does not add analytics or advertising scripts.

Your choices and questions

You can keep transcription on-device, decline a cloud-upload prompt, remove a provider key, delete a local recording, and choose whether to share it. If applicable law gives you rights concerning data handled through a support request, email alextkint@me.com with the request. For audio already sent to a provider using your account, consult that provider's privacy controls and contact them as needed; we do not have an app-operated copy or access to your provider account. We may update this page as the app or providers change, with a new effective date.

Recording other people may require their consent. Transcripts and summaries can be inaccurate; check them before use or sharing.
